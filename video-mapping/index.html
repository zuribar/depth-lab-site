Projection Mapping

Projection Mapping and Multi-Projector Setups in Israel

DepthLab, the Tel Aviv studio of Zori Baazov, plans and runs projection mapping, also called video mapping, for venues, malls, brands, event producers, festivals, artists and museums across Israel. The work covers the whole chain: site survey, projector and lens planning, blending several projectors into one image, mapping the content to the real surface and calibration on site. Projects include three immersive VIP rooms at an entertainment venue, with five to six projectors per room, and a building facade at Hasifa Music Festival in Jerusalem.

What's included

  • Site survey and measurement of the surface or room
  • Projector and lens planning, with equipment through suppliers
  • Blending several projectors into one continuous image
  • Content mapped to the geometry and viewing position of the space
  • Mapping on stage sets and objects for live shows
  • Calibration on site
  • Live operation from a control desk, or unattended playback

How it works

  1. Site survey

    We visit the space, measure the surface and check the light, the power and where projectors can go.

  2. Projector plan

    Projectors and lenses are chosen for the image size and the throw distance.

  3. Content

    Content is made to the measured geometry, so it sits on the real surface.

  4. Install and calibrate

    The projectors go up, and the image is blended, mapped and calibrated on site.

  5. Show or handover

    The mapping runs live from a control desk, or plays on its own in a permanent setup.

Frequently asked questions

What is projection mapping (video mapping)?

Projection mapping, also called 3D projection mapping, is video made to fit the measured shape of a real surface, such as a room, a stage set, an object or a building facade. When one projector is not enough, several projectors are blended into one image. DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio, plans the whole chain, from site survey to calibration on site.

Who does projection mapping in Israel?

DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio founded by Zori Baazov, plans and runs projection mapping across Israel, from immersive rooms to stage pieces and a building facade. Projects include three VIP rooms at an entertainment venue, with five to six projectors per room, and facade mapping at Hasifa Music Festival in Jerusalem.

What is the difference between video art and projection mapping?

Video art is the visual content itself, shown on screens or in a projection. Projection mapping fits that content to the shape of a real surface, such as a wall, a stage set or a building, so it sits on the surface exactly. DepthLab makes both: the content, and the mapping that fits it to the surface.

Can you map video onto a stage set or objects?

Yes. For Omri Smadar's party, the studio designed the stage and mapped video onto an inflatable moon and a carved wooden log, with real-time generative scenes. Stage projection mapping starts with measuring the objects, so the content fits their real shape.

Can you do projection mapping for a festival or a city event?

Yes. DepthLab mapped a building facade at Hasifa Music Festival in Jerusalem and ran it live from a control desk. A festival or city project starts with a site survey of the surface and the event's schedule, since the mapping can only run after dark.

Does projection mapping work outdoors or in daylight?

Projection needs darkness. Outdoors, a mapping show works after sunset, and the more ambient light there is, the more projector brightness is needed. For daylight, LED screens are the usual choice.

What is multi-projector blending?

Blending, or edge blending, joins the images of several projectors into one seamless picture: the edges overlap and fade into each other, so a large wall or a whole room reads as one image. In the VIP rooms DepthLab designed, five to six projectors per room cover three walls, about 270 degrees. How many projectors a surface needs depends on its size, the throw distance and the ambient light, which the site survey checks.

Which software do you use for projection mapping?

The studio's tools include Resolume Arena, TouchDesigner, Smode and Unreal Engine 5, with timecode when the mapping follows music. The choice depends on the surface, the content, and whether the mapping runs live from a control desk or plays on its own.

What does the venue need to provide for video mapping?

The venue needs to give access for a site survey, a safe place to mount the projectors at the right distance from the surface, power, and darkness during the show. To start, send photos, measurements or plans of the surface, and the dates. Projectors and lenses can come through suppliers as part of the plan.

How long does it take to prepare a projection mapping project?

It depends on the surface and on how much new content is needed. Every project needs a site survey, a projector plan, and setup and calibration time on site; content made for the geometry adds production time. Send the date early so the plan can work back from it.

How much does projection mapping cost?

DepthLab does not publish prices, because each project is different. The main cost factors are the size of the surface, the throw distance, the ambient light, the number of projectors, the length of the content and the number of setup and show days.
