Zori Baazov (זורי באזוב), a VJ and visual artist based in Tel Aviv, makes audiovisual shows for musicians and live visuals for clubs and festivals across Israel. He founded DepthLab (Depth Lab Studio), a studio for live visuals, projection mapping and interactive installations, from stage shows to permanent rooms.

Zori was born in Georgia and moved to Israel. He studied New Media at Musrara, the Naggar School of Art and Society, in Jerusalem: creative coding, video and sensor-based installations.

He started as a VJ in clubs, at parties and fashion shows. Later he worked as a VJ and real-time operator on large concert productions: LED screens, projection, on-stage troubleshooting, and working with directors, lighting designers and artists.

Since founding DepthLab, he has worked directly with clients, taking projects from site survey to handover: specification, system design (projectors, LED, media servers, sensors), suppliers and budget, content, installation and calibration.

On stage he mostly works with electronic music. He wants the visuals to follow the arrangement of a set, its drops and breakdowns, and to react to the frequencies as well.

For Daskal, a keyboardist, composer and producer, he built a timecode show that turns the live set into one audiovisual show, and he runs it live at every show. For Omri Smadar's live shows he made audio-reactive visuals for each song with a live closed-circuit camera. For Omri Smadar's party he designed the stage and mapped video with real-time generative scenes onto an inflatable moon and a carved wooden log.

He uses AI every day, including AI coding agents that automate parts of his pipeline, such as Unreal scripting, rendering and encoding. His R&D includes a real-time AI mirror. In development: an immersive rehabilitation room for a hospital's clinical team, with markerless body tracking on depth cameras.

DepthLab is open to studio projects for entertainment venues, malls, museums, events and brands across Israel. Zori is also open to roles in real-time and interactive experiences, live production and multimedia project management.
