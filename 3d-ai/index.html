Content, 3D & AI

Content, 3D and AI for LED Screens and Projection in Israel

DepthLab, the Tel Aviv studio of Zori Baazov, makes custom content for LED screens, projection mapping and immersive rooms, for venues, artists and musicians, event producers and brands across Israel. The work covers seamless loops, 3D animation and worlds in Unreal Engine 5 and Blender, real-time generative scenes and AI-generated content, produced on local and cloud pipelines. Each piece is made for the real screen or surface, at its resolution and geometry. For the VIP rooms at a bowling complex in Kiryat Gat, the studio made sixteen visuals, mostly in Unreal Engine, rendered as one wide canvas of 5120 x 800 px per room.

What's included

  • Seamless loops made for your venue's screens or walls, ready to run daily on a media server
  • 3D worlds and animation in Unreal Engine 5 and Blender, for projection and LED screens
  • Scenes generated in real time for live shows and stage mapping
  • AI-generated content on local and cloud pipelines
  • Content adapted to the geometry and viewing position of each space

How it works

  1. Canvas

    We start from the real screen or wall: resolution, shape and where people watch from.

  2. Look

    References and test frames set the look before full production.

  3. Production

    3D, AI and motion work, rendered at the full size of the canvas or built to run in real time during a show.

  4. Delivery

    Rendered loops are encoded and tested on the media server; real-time scenes are set up and tested in the space.

More about this service

Rendered in advance or generated live

Some content is rendered in advance and plays as seamless loops, like the sixteen visuals that run daily in the bowling complex's VIP rooms. Other content is generated live while the show runs. For a singer's live shows, the studio made audio-reactive visuals for each song with a live closed-circuit camera, and later mapped video onto an inflatable moon and a carved wooden log on stage, with real-time generative scenes.

Frequently asked questions

Who creates content for LED screens and projection in Israel?

DepthLab, the Tel Aviv studio of Zori Baazov, creates custom content for LED screens, projection mapping and immersive rooms across Israel, from seamless loops to 3D worlds and AI-generated content. For a bowling complex in Kiryat Gat, it made sixteen visuals for three VIP rooms, each adapted to the geometry and viewing position of its room.

Who makes 3D animation for projection or LED screens in Israel?

DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio, makes 3D animation and worlds in Unreal Engine 5 and Blender for projection, LED screens and immersive rooms. The Kiryat Gat worlds were built mostly in Unreal Engine, with a camera rig matched to the measured walls, and rendered as one wide canvas per room.

What resolution should content be for a wide multi-projector canvas?

The content is made at the size of the full canvas the projectors cover once they are blended. In Kiryat Gat, each room is one canvas of 5120 x 800 px across three walls. The exact size comes from the projector plan for your space.

What is the difference between pre-rendered content and real-time generative visuals?

Pre-rendered content is made before the event and plays back as video, often as seamless loops on a media server. Real-time generative visuals are calculated by software while the show runs, so they can change with the music or the moment. DepthLab makes both: rendered loops for permanent rooms, and live scenes mapped onto objects on stage for a singer's live shows.

Can AI-generated video be used on venue screens or in a live show?

Yes. The studio produces AI-generated content on local and cloud pipelines and prepares it for the real screen or surface like any other content, at the right resolution and format. Zori uses AI every day, including AI coding agents that automate parts of the pipeline, such as Unreal scripting, rendering and encoding.

Can you make seamless loops that a venue plays every day?

Yes. The Kiryat Gat rooms run seamless loops daily in Resolume Arena. Venue staff choose them, and no operator is needed.

What should I send to start a content project?

Send the screen or projector layout with resolutions, photos or plans of the space, where people watch from, the theme or references you have in mind, and the date it must be ready. If the setup is not planned yet, the studio can plan it together with the content.

How much does custom content cost?

DepthLab does not publish prices. The cost depends on the number and length of the pieces, the size of the canvas, the amount of 3D or AI work, and how many versions the space needs.
