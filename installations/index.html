Immersive Rooms

Immersive Rooms and Permanent Installations in Israel

DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio, designs and builds immersive projection rooms and permanent installations for entertainment venues, malls, museums and brands across Israel. An immersive room surrounds the viewer with projection on several walls. The studio takes each room from site survey to handover, and the finished system can run every day without an operator, as in the three VIP rooms it delivered at a bowling complex in Kiryat Gat in 2026.

What's included

  • Site survey, specification and system design: projectors, LED, media servers and sensors
  • Suppliers and budget
  • Content made for each room's geometry and viewing position
  • Installation, blending and calibration
  • Automated playback that venue staff run without an operator
  • Themed worlds for kids, teens and adults

How it works

  1. Site survey

    We measure the room and the viewing positions, and check the light and the power.

  2. Specification

    Projectors, media server, suppliers and budget are planned and agreed.

  3. Content

    Worlds are made for the measured walls, for example in Unreal Engine with a camera rig matched to the room.

  4. Install and calibrate

    Projectors are installed, and the image is blended and calibrated in the room.

  5. Handover

    Playback is automated, and venue staff choose the content without an operator.

More about this service

Who it is for

Immersive rooms suit entertainment venues, such as bowling complexes and family entertainment centers, and malls, museums and brands that want a permanent immersive experience. In Kiryat Gat, more than a dozen themed worlds serve kids, teens and adults, and venue staff choose them without an operator. For the same kinds of spaces, the studio is also developing real-time AI installations, starting with an AI mirror that is still in R&D.

Frequently asked questions

What is an immersive room?

An immersive room is a space where projection covers several walls around the viewer, so the image surrounds the people in the room. In DepthLab's installation at a bowling complex in Kiryat Gat, each VIP room wraps the viewer on three walls, about 270 degrees.

What is the difference between an immersive room and an interactive room?

In an immersive room, projection covers several walls and surrounds the people inside, as in the three VIP rooms DepthLab delivered in Kiryat Gat. In an interactive room, the content also responds to the people, through depth cameras, LiDAR or body tracking. The two can be combined in one space.

Who builds immersive projection rooms in Israel?

DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio founded by Zori Baazov, designs and builds immersive projection rooms and permanent installations across Israel, from site survey to handover. In 2026 it delivered three VIP rooms at a bowling complex in Kiryat Gat, with five to six projectors per room and sixteen visuals, which run every day without an operator.

How many projectors does a three-wall room need?

It depends on the size of the room, the throw distance and how bright the image must be. In Kiryat Gat, each VIP room uses five to six projectors to cover three walls, about 270 degrees. A site survey gives the real number for your room.

Can two rooms be joined into one image?

Yes. In DepthLab's installation at a bowling complex in Kiryat Gat, two of the three VIP rooms can open into one continuous image.

Can a permanent installation run without an operator?

Yes. In Kiryat Gat, seamless loops run daily in Resolume Arena as the permanent runtime. Venue staff choose the loops, and no operator is needed.

Can one room have different worlds for kids, teens and adults?

Yes. The Kiryat Gat installation has sixteen visuals, more than a dozen themed worlds for kids, teens and adults, including Jungle, Casino, Mansion, Sky City, Football, City Sunset and Hero City. Venue staff choose which world plays.

What does a venue need to prepare for a site survey?

For a site survey, the venue gives access to the room and sends its measurements or plans, the places people will watch from, and details about the light and the power. The survey checks where projectors can go and how the image can reach every wall.

How long does an immersive room project take?

It depends on the number of rooms and how much content is made. For reference, the three VIP rooms in Kiryat Gat, with sixteen visuals, were delivered between June and September 2026.

How much does an immersive projection room cost?

DepthLab does not publish prices. The cost depends on the size and number of rooms, the number of projectors, the media server, how many worlds are made, and the installation and calibration work.
