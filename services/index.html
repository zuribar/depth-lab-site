Services for shows, venues and events in Israel

DepthLab is a Tel Aviv studio that offers six services to musicians, event producers, venues and brands across Israel: live visuals and VJ, projection mapping, immersive rooms, interactive installations, content and 3D, and real-time AI. Each service can stand on its own or be part of one project, from site survey to handover.

  • Live Visuals & VJ

    Show design, content synced to the music via timecode, audio-reactive visuals and live operation.

  • Projection Mapping & Multi-Projector Setups

    Rooms, stage sets and objects: site survey, projector and lens planning, blending, mapping and calibration.

  • LED Installations & LED Stages

    3D LED pieces and LED stages: content built for the real pixel map, simulated before the build and run live from TouchDesigner.

  • Permanent Installations & Immersive Rooms

    Design, equipment, installation, automation and handover. Runs without an operator.

  • Interactive & Sensors

    Installations and interactive content that respond to people, with depth cameras, LiDAR and body tracking.

  • Content, 3D & AI

    Custom loops, 3D worlds, generative scenes and AI content, made for the real screen or wall.

  • Real-Time AI

    A real-time AI mirror in R&D: live AI video from a camera on one GPU, for venues, malls and museums.

Who the services are for

  • Musicians and artists

    DepthLab turns a live set into an audiovisual show, as in Daskal's timecode show, run live by Zori at every date, and Omri Smadar's live shows, with audio-reactive visuals and a live camera. For Omri Smadar's party, DepthLab designed the stage and made custom video mapping for it. The studio also creates interactive content that responds to the people in the room.

    Live visuals for club nights, festivals and events with several stages, from show design to live operation. Zori ran the visuals for three stages in parallel at White Night 2025 at the Tel Aviv Museum of Art, and played live visuals as VJ at club nights at Oman 17 in Tel Aviv.
