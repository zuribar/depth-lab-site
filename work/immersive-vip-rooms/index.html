Immersive VIP Room Design

DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio, designed three immersive VIP rooms at an entertainment venue. Each room wraps the viewer in projection on three walls, about 270 degrees, and two of the rooms can open into one picture. Sixteen visuals run there every day as seamless loops, built and rendered mostly in Unreal Engine to the real wall sizes, the projector layout and the resolution of the rooms. The venue staff choose them with no operator.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): content, mapping and playback system

In the room

The worlds

About the project

Made for the real walls

Each room has five to six projectors, blended into one image across the three walls. The visuals are made to the real wall sizes, the projector layout and the resolution. The worlds were built mostly in Unreal Engine, with a camera rig matched to the measured walls, and every visual is rendered as one wide canvas of 5120 x 800 px per room and adapted to the geometry and viewing position of that room.

The worlds

The content is sixteen visuals, more than a dozen themed worlds for kids, teens and adults: among them a magic forest, a jungle and an ice world for kids; open space, a spaceship cockpit and a space station for ages 9 to 14; and a mansion, a casino, a city at sunset and a city in the sky for adults. The gallery shows the rooms during the installation, and the worlds as full rendered canvases.

Zori's role and daily playback

Zori Baazov of DepthLab made the content, mapped it to the rooms and set up the playback system. Seamless loops now run there daily in Resolume Arena as the permanent runtime. The venue staff choose which world plays, and no operator is needed.
