Immersive VIP Rooms, Bowling Complex, Kiryat Gat

2026 · Bowling complex · Kiryat Gat

DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio, turned three VIP rooms at a bowling complex in Kiryat Gat into permanent immersive projection rooms in 2026. Each room wraps the viewer on three walls, about 270 degrees, and two of the rooms can open into one continuous image. Sixteen visuals run there every day as seamless loops, and the venue staff choose them with no operator.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): content, mapping and playback system
Year
2026, delivered between June and September
Venue
A bowling complex, three VIP rooms
Location
Kiryat Gat
About the project

How it works

Each room has five to six projectors, blended into one image across the three walls. The worlds were built mostly in Unreal Engine, with a camera rig matched to the measured walls. Every visual is rendered as one wide canvas of 5120 x 800 px per room and adapted to the geometry and viewing position of that room.

The worlds

The content is sixteen visuals, more than a dozen themed worlds for kids, teens and adults, among them Jungle, Casino, Mansion, Sky City, Football, City Sunset and Hero City. The gallery shows them as rendered canvases: in the panoramas, a whole room is laid out flat, and the jungle runs from the plants on the left wall to the waterfall on the right.

Zori's role and daily playback

Zori Baazov of DepthLab made the content, mapped it to the rooms and set up the playback system. The rooms were delivered between June and September 2026, and seamless loops now run daily in Resolume Arena as the permanent runtime. The venue staff choose which world plays, and no operator is needed.
