Audio-Reactive Visuals and Stage Mapping for a Singer's Live Shows

2025-2026 · A singer's live shows

For a singer's live shows in December 2025 and September 2026, DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio, made audio-reactive visuals for each song with a live closed-circuit camera, and later mapped video onto an inflatable moon and a carved wooden log on stage, with real-time generative scenes.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): visuals for each song, live camera and stage video mapping
Year
December 2025 and September 2026
Venue
A singer's live shows
About the project

Visuals for each song

Each song in the shows had its own visuals, made to react to the audio as the song is played. A live closed-circuit camera was part of the setup.

Projection mapping on stage objects

Later, the studio mapped video onto two objects on the stage: an inflatable moon and a carved wooden log. The scenes on them were generative and ran in real time during the show.

How it works

Audio-reactive visuals take their movement from the sound, so the image follows the song as it is played. Mapping onto objects starts from their real shape, so the content sits on the object itself rather than on a flat screen.

For musicians

For musicians, a show like this combines two DepthLab services: live visuals that respond to the music, and video mapping on the stage set. The songs, the visuals and the objects on stage then come together as one audiovisual show.
