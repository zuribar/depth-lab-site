Omri Smadar's Party: Stage Design and Video Mapping

For Omri Smadar's party, DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio, designed the stage and made custom video mapping for it: an inflatable moon and a carved wooden log on stage, mapped with real-time generative looks, together with Erez Cohen. Earlier, for Omri Smadar's live shows, Zori Baazov made audio-reactive visuals for each song with a live closed-circuit camera.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): stage design, video mapping and visuals
About the project

Stage design and video mapping

For Omri Smadar's party, DepthLab designed the stage and made custom video mapping for it. The video was mapped onto two objects on the stage, an inflatable moon and a carved wooden log. The looks on them were generative and ran in real time during the party. The stage mapping was made together with Erez Cohen.

Visuals for each song

Earlier, for Omri Smadar's live shows, each song had its own visuals, made to react to the audio as the song is played. A live closed-circuit camera was part of the setup.

How it works

Mapping onto objects starts from their real shape, so the content sits on the object itself rather than on a flat screen. Audio-reactive visuals take their movement from the sound, so the image follows the song as it is played.

For musicians and event producers

A project like this combines stage design with two DepthLab services: video mapping on the stage set, and live visuals that respond to the music. The stage, the objects on it and the visuals then come together as one audiovisual show.
