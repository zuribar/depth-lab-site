LAB: Experimental Video Mapping

2026 · DepthLab LAB · Tel Aviv

LAB is where Zori Baazov of DepthLab tries new kinds of video mapping on real objects: a carved relief, objects on a table, stage pieces. Each one is mapped so the picture sits on its real shape. Veins grow out of a relief, a mandala settles on its carved rings, and stone seems to come alive. The same experiments go into shows, like the moon and the carved log at Omri Smadar's party.

About the project

On the relief

On a carved relief at the studio, the looks grow out of the relief's own rings and edges: veins that grow from the center, a mandala on the carved rings, stone that seems to come alive. Each part of the relief can get its own content and light.

How the shape is measured

A projector throws patterns of light and a camera reads them, so every object gets a measured mask and depth that go straight into TouchDesigner. If something moves, a new scan maps it again.

From LAB to the stage

The same experiments go into shows, like the inflatable moon and the carved wooden log at Omri Smadar's party.
