LAB: Experiments

LAB is where Zori runs his experiments, and then tries to bring them into the real world: LEDs that follow his hand, live closed-circuit cameras, real-time AI, generative visuals and video mapping on real objects. Some have already reached the stage, like the moon and the carved log at Omri Smadar's party.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): experiments and prototypes
About the project

Hand control

While the LED cube was being built at home, a sphere of light inside the hanging LEDs followed a hand up and down. On a building facade, the projected light followed a raised arm.

Closed circuit

Live camera feeds become the material: the picture from closed-circuit cameras is glitched and rewound in real time and shown on a giant screen above the DJ booth.

Real-time AI

In real-time AI tests, a living room turns into a space station, and the person walking through it into an astronaut.

Generative visuals

Looks from Zori's TouchDesigner deck, like a black disc with a corona of light filaments around it, are tried here first.

Video mapping on objects

On a carved relief at the studio, the looks grow out of the relief's own rings and edges. A projector throws patterns of light and a camera reads them, so every object gets a measured mask and depth that go straight into TouchDesigner.

From LAB to the real world

The experiments go out into shows and installations: the inflatable moon and the carved wooden log at Omri Smadar's party, the LED tower that was first seen in a 3D preview and then played live, and the LED cube at Midburn.
