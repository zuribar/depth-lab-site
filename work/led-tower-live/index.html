Live 3D LED Tower

A 2.5 m LED tower of four LED modules, about 20,400 pixels, that Zori played live from his TouchDesigner deck. Each module was placed in 3D the way it really stood and checked pixel by pixel, so shapes like a ball or rising fire move through the whole volume, not across a flat screen.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): pixel mapping, LED looks and live operation
About the project

The tower

Four LED modules stand together as one tower, 2.5 m tall. Every LED has a fixed place in 3D, so a look is drawn into the volume: a ball can float inside it and fire can rise through it.

From the preview to the real LEDs

On site, a mapping tool placed every module in 3D the way it really stood, sent test patterns to check every row and pixel, and handed the map to TouchDesigner. The ball seen in the tool's 3D preview then appeared on the real LEDs.

Live

Zori ran the looks live from his TouchDesigner deck, with each module on its own Art-Net controller.

Type
3D LED installation, played live
Size
Four LED modules, 0.5 × 0.5 m each and 2.5 m tall, standing as one tower
Pixels
About 20,400 (5,100 per module)
Control
TouchDesigner and Art-Net, one controller per module
