Facade Mapping at Hasifa Music Festival

For Hasifa Music Festival, DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio, mapped video onto a building facade. The mapping was run live from a control desk during the festival.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): video mapping, run live from a control desk
About the project

The facade

The content was fitted to the shape of the building, across its floors and around its corner, so the projection follows the architecture rather than a flat screen.

Live from the control desk

At the control desk, the screen shows a node-based patch, a timecode readout and a preview of the facade content, and a laptop shows the mapping layout, with the stage lights behind it. From there, the mapping was run live during the International Music Showcase Festival (Hasifa), produced by the Yellow Submarine.

On the building

At night the gallery shows black-and-white patterns across the floors and around a blue-lit floor, a large pink illustration on the corner of the building, colorful geometric shapes above the festival crowd and a sparkling purple projection above the stage. A wide view across the rooftops shows the mapped facade with the city lights behind it.

How a facade mapping is prepared

A facade mapping needs darkness, a safe place for the projectors at the right distance from the building, and content made to its measured shape. DepthLab's projection mapping page describes the full process, from the site survey to calibration on site.
