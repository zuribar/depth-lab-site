LED DJ Stage, Designed in Unreal Engine

A DJ stage of LED cubes, the same model as Zori's real LED modules, built as a simulation in Unreal Engine with every LED in place. Zori plays his deck's looks on it, keys the club lights (beams, strobes and blinders) to the same music and renders the show, so a producer can see the stage, the content and the lights together before anything is built. The stage itself doesn't exist yet.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): stage design, LED looks, light cues and rendering
About the project

Layouts

The cubes stand around the DJ in different layouts: a ring, a horizon line, an arch or a gate with a screen, each with its own looks. The cube model is the same as Zori's real LED modules, so every look is made for real hardware.

Content and light together

The LED looks and the club lights follow one score measured to the music, so drops, cuts to black and light hits land together in the render.

Before the build

The stage exists as a simulation and rendered video, ready for a producer or a venue that wants to build it.
