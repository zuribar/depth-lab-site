Visuals for Three Stages at White Night, Tel Aviv Museum of Art

At White Night (Laila Lavan) at the Tel Aviv Museum of Art, Zori Baazov of DepthLab ran the visuals for three stages in parallel. Separately, Daskal, a keyboardist, composer and producer, performed there with the timecode show Zori built for his set.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): ran the visuals for three stages; supplier coordination, content management, coordination of the lighting artists and video artists
About the project

The event

On White Night, the Tel Aviv Museum of Art had three stages running at the same time. In the gallery, the stages carry projection on their walls, with hanging LED columns and light strands above them.

Zori's role

Running the visuals for three stages at once was production work as well as content work. Zori coordinated the suppliers, managed the content for the stages, and coordinated the lighting artists and video artists across all three.

Daskal's set

Daskal's set used the timecode show Zori built for him, which Zori operates live at every date. On that stage, Daskal played keyboards in front of a long wall of projected line drawings and clouds, with spiral patterns on the LED columns above.

Day and night

At 09:00 the rig was up on the stage wall in daylight, and at 21:00 the same wall was part of the show. By day, the gallery shows the white wall, the truss with its hanging LED columns, the speakers and the control desk on the lawn; by night, the stages glow with a projected mandala, a swirling purple wash and spiral patterns on the LED columns.
