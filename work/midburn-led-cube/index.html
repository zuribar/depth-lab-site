LED Cube Installation at Midburn

Zori Baazov of DepthLab, a Tel Aviv studio, built an LED cube at home, in his living room, and lit it in the desert at Midburn. At night, the people around the cube became part of the visuals.

Role
Zori Baazov (DepthLab): built the LED cube at home and lit it in the desert
About the project

The build

The cube started as a home build in Zori's living room. A clip from the build shows hanging LED strands with a glowing white sphere, tested by hand, before the cube went to the desert.

At Midburn

At Midburn, the cube stood in the desert on its frame, with road cases at its base. At night it was lit with flowing colors and people gathered around it, and one clip shows a dancer with a light-up hoop in front of it.

What the clips show

The clips show human figures forming in the cube's lights. The cube glows purple in the dark with a figure made of its lights; a dancer raises an arm as a figure appears; a woman spreads her arms and a figure in the lights takes the same pose as the colors change; and a silhouette in a fur coat faces the cube as a figure forms.

Type
LED cube installation
System

Pixels

Interaction
A depth camera detects the people around the cube and turns them into points of light on the LEDs, all in TouchDesigner
Runtime
At night, during Midburn
