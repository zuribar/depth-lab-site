LED

LED installations and LED stages

Zori builds looks for LED pieces where every pixel has its place in 3D, from a 2.5 m LED tower he played live to a cube at Midburn that reacted to the people around it. The looks are prepared on a 3D model of the real LEDs, so shapes, colours and timing are set before the night, and then run live from his TouchDesigner deck over Art-Net. For stages that are not built yet, he designs the whole set in Unreal Engine: the LEDs, the club lights and the music together. He also makes content for LED screens and LED walls at shows, clubs and events.

What's included

  • Looks built for the real pixel map: 3D content that reads from every side of the piece
  • A 3D model of every LED, to design and check the show before the build
  • Live operation from TouchDesigner over Art-Net, with cues for the set
  • Interactive LED pieces that respond to people, with depth cameras
  • LED stage and DJ booth concepts in Unreal Engine, rendered with the club lights and the music

How it works

  1. Brief

    The space, the music and the LED hardware: module sizes, pixel counts and controllers.

  2. Pixel map

    Every module is placed in 3D the way it really stands, and test patterns check every row and pixel.

  3. Looks

    Content and cues are built on the 3D model and checked from the crowd's point of view.

  4. Live

    On the night the looks run on the real LEDs, operated live.

Frequently asked questions

Who makes content for LED installations and 3D LED in Israel?

DepthLab, the Tel Aviv studio of VJ Zori Baazov, builds looks for 3D LED pieces and runs them live from TouchDesigner, from a 2.5 m LED tower to an interactive LED cube at Midburn.

Can I see an LED stage before it is built?

Yes. Zori models the stage and every LED in Unreal Engine, keys the club lights to the same music and renders the show, so the design and the content can be checked before anything is built. The LED DJ stage on this site exists only as such a simulation for now.

Do you need to know the LED hardware in advance?

Yes: module sizes, pixel counts and controllers. The looks are built for the real pixel map, so on site the mapping is checked and the show runs as designed.

Can the LEDs react to people or to the music?

Yes. With depth cameras the LEDs can respond to the people around them, as with the LED cube at Midburn, and looks can follow the music live.
