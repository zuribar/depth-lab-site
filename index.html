What we do

Services→
  • Live Visuals & VJShow design, content synced to the music via timecode, audio-reactive visuals and live operation.
  • Projection MappingRooms, stage sets and objects: site survey, projector and lens planning, blending, mapping and calibration.
  • LED3D LED pieces and LED stages: content built for the real pixel map, simulated before the build and run live from TouchDesigner.
  • Immersive RoomsDesign, equipment, installation, automation and handover. Runs without an operator.
  • Interactive & SensorsInstallations and interactive content that respond to people, with depth cameras, LiDAR and body tracking.
  • Content, 3D & AICustom loops, 3D worlds, generative scenes and AI content, made for the real screen or wall.
  • Real-Time AIA real-time AI mirror in R&D: live AI video from a camera on one GPU, for venues, malls and museums.
